Mevreon Bio-AI Platform — Technical Walkthrough Dossier Section 1: Architecture & Scope
01. Campaign Architecture & Pathogen Target Coverage Platform Scope
Screening campaign designed to overcome antimicrobial resistance (AMR) by identifying non-traditional virulence and efflux targets in priority gram-negative and gram-positive pathogens.
WHO Critical Priority Bacterial Pathogens Target Discovery
The platform integrates 12 validated target proteins across three clinically dangerous AMR pathogens:
  • Pseudomonas aeruginosa: Quorum sensing master regulators (PqsR/MvfR, LasR), biofilm polysaccharide biosynthesis (PelD), and RND efflux (MexB).
  • Staphylococcus aureus: Virulence response regulator (AgrA), cell wall anchoring transpeptidase (Sortase A / SrtA), and methicillin-resistance PBP2a (MecA).
  • Enterobacteriaceae (K. pneumoniae): AcrB multidrug efflux pump, MurA peptidoglycan biosynthesis, and porin OmpK36.
5-Stage Preclinical Evaluation Framework Methodology
Every phytochemical undergoes a rigorous five-step biophysical and computational audit: (1) AutoDock Vina Thermodynamic ΔG Grid Docking • (2) DiffDock-L Equivariant Diffusion Spatial Pose Prediction • (3) Atomic Contact & Non-Covalent Interaction Parsing • (4) Mechanism of Action Downstream Phenotypic Cascade Routing • (5) Multi-Parametric Validation Priority Score Generation (0-100).
